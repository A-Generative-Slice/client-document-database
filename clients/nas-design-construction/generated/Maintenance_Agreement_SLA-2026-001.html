2. Scope of Services

2.1 Hosting & Infrastructure Management

A Generative Slice shall provide and maintain the following services throughout the term of this agreement:

Web Hosting Services Complete hosting infrastructure management including server resources, bandwidth allocation, and uptime monitoring
Server Maintenance Regular server updates, performance optimization, and configuration management
Security Management Implementation of security patches, SSL certificate maintenance, and basic security monitoring
Backup Services Routine automated backups of website files and databases, with secure off-site storage
Uptime Monitoring Proactive monitoring to ensure maximum website availability

2.2 Content Update Services

The Client is entitled to request minor manual content updates through A Generative Slice, subject to the following terms:

  • Monthly Request Allowance: Up to 4 content update requests per calendar month
  • Eligible Updates: Text modifications, image replacements, contact information updates, gallery additions, and similar content-level changes
  • Turnaround Time: 48-72 business hours from receipt of clear, written update instructions
  • Update Requests: All requests must be submitted via email to agenerativeslice@gmail.com with clear specifications

Unused monthly requests do not roll over to subsequent months.

3. Service Exclusions

The following services are explicitly outside the scope of this Maintenance Agreement and will require a separate project scope, quotation, and invoicing:

3.1 Major Structural Changes

  • Complete website redesigns or significant layout modifications
  • Changes to the website's fundamental architecture or navigation structure
  • Integration of new third-party platforms or services

3.2 New Feature Development

  • Development of custom functionality or interactive features
  • Addition of e-commerce capabilities, user authentication systems, or databases
  • Implementation of advanced plugins or custom coding requirements

3.3 New Page Creation

  • Design and development of entirely new website pages or sections
  • Creation of new page templates or custom layouts

3.4 Third-Party Services

  • Costs associated with premium plugins, themes, or third-party software licenses
  • Fees for external services such as email marketing platforms or analytics tools

Should the Client require any excluded services, A Generative Slice will provide a separate written quotation. Work will commence only upon written approval and payment of the additional fees.

4. Service Level Commitments

4.1 Uptime Guarantee

A Generative Slice shall make commercially reasonable efforts to maintain website uptime of 99.5% or higher, excluding scheduled maintenance windows and circumstances beyond our control.

4.2 Response Time

  • Critical Issues (Website Crash): System triage response within 4 business hours. Full backend recovery and online fix-back execution completed within 48 to 72 business hours from formal notification.
  • Non-Critical Issues (minor bugs, content updates): Response within 48 business hours

4.3 Business Hours

Business hours are defined as 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays in India.

5. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  • Provide clear, written instructions for all content update requests
  • Supply all necessary materials (text, images, documents) in web-ready formats when possible
  • Maintain the confidentiality of any access credentials provided by A Generative Slice
  • Refrain from making unauthorized changes to the website's hosting or technical infrastructure
  • Respond promptly to requests for clarification or approval

Hosting Ownership Isolation: The Client retains full administrative ownership of their Hostinger environment. Any server downtime or security breaks caused by the Client (or an unverified third party) making direct, unauthorized changes to the DNS settings, server properties, or source files will completely void the 48-72 hour recovery guarantees. Fixing such incidents will be billed separately.
