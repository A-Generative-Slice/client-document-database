1. Purpose & Scope

This Mutual Non-Disclosure and Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between A Generative Slice and Light Lab to ensure the absolute confidentiality and secure handling of proprietary workflows, data pipelines, technical parameters, and business secrets exchanged during pair programming, AI systems auditing, and localized tool development.

2. Confidential Information & Data Protection

2.1 Strict Confidentiality Mandate

A Generative Slice agrees to maintain strict, uncompromising confidentiality regarding Light Lab's proprietary workflows, BigQuery (BQ) parameters, database schemas, internal algorithms, source code, and client data disclosed during on-site or remote engagements.

2.2 Standard of Care

Both parties agree to protect the other's Confidential Information using the exact same standard of care they apply to their own most valuable trade secrets, but in no event less than a reasonable commercial standard of care.

3. Closed-Loop Processing Mandate

Closed-Loop AI Processing: No proprietary data, BQ parameters, code snippets, or workflows processed during this engagement will ever be used to train, fine-tune, prompt, or enrich external foundational LLMs outside of Light Lab’s specific, approved cloud infrastructure and isolated enterprise tenant environments.

All AI-assisted auditing and localized tool development must occur strictly within closed-loop pipelines approved by the Client's technical leadership.

4. Intellectual Property (IP) Ownership

4.1 Code & Tool Ownership

Light Lab retains full, unencumbered ownership of all localized tools, custom scripts, architecture designs, and codebase generated by A Generative Slice during the scheduled on-site days and retainer engagements.

4.2 Condition Precedent

Full transfer and assignment of Intellectual Property ownership to Light Lab is strictly contingent upon full and timely payment of all monthly invoices and retainer fees issued by A Generative Slice.

5. Term & Survival

This Agreement shall become effective on June 8, 2026 and shall apply to all disclosures made during the active retainer period. The confidentiality obligations, data protection mandates, and IP ownership terms established herein shall survive the termination or expiration of the primary service engagement for a period of five (5) years.

6. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any legal action or dispute arising from this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in India.
