2. Scope of Services & On-Site Retainer

2.1 Core AI Auditor Scope

Service Provider (A Generative Slice) will dedicate exactly two (2) on-site days per week as an AI Auditor for localized tool development and workflow optimization for Light Lab.

Localized Tool Development Custom AI tool architecture, auditing, and localized workflow automation for internal operations.
AI Systems Auditing Evaluation and optimization of existing proprietary models, BQ parameters, and data pipelines.
On-Site Consultation Direct technical guidance and pair programming during scheduled on-site days at Light Lab HQ.

2.2 Scheduling & Work Arrangement

  • Weekly Scheduling Confirmation: Client must confirm the 2 working days by the preceding Sunday via WhatsApp or telephone call.
  • Default Schedule: If unnotified by Sunday evening, the default working days for the week will automatically be set to Friday and Saturday.
  • Work From Home (WFH): Any remote Work From Home arrangement is strictly at the Service Provider's discretion.

2.3 Working Hours & Time Allocation

  • Daily Hours: Scheduled working hours are exactly 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (inclusive of a 1-hour lunch break).
  • Hours Mandate: The Client cannot mandate or require work outside of these stipulated hours.

2.4 Missed Days & Extra Days Policy

  • Missed Days Make-Up: Any missed days by the Service Provider will be made up within the same week or the subsequent week.
  • Extra Days Billing: Any extra on-site or working days demanded by the Client beyond the standard 8-day monthly retainer scope will be billed at a pro-rated per diem rate of ₹1,250 INR per day.

3. Service Exclusions

The following services are explicitly outside the scope of this agreement and require a separate project handover contract and quotation:

3.1 Large-Scale Projects & Web Integrations

  • Primary Website 3D & GLB Integrations: Large-scale projects (e.g., primary website 3D Three.js/GLB chatbot integration) are strictly excluded from this retainer.
  • Major Architectural Rewrites: Enterprise-wide structural refactoring or external customer-facing product launches require a separate project handover contract.

3.2 Infrastructure & API Costs

  • Third-Party Costs: All third-party API usage fees, cloud infrastructure billing, LLM token costs, and external software licenses are excluded from the retainer and must be paid directly by the Client.

Should the Client require any excluded services (such as 3D Three.js/GLB chatbot integration), A Generative Slice will provide a separate project handover contract. Work will commence only upon execution of that contract.

4. Service Level Commitments

4.1 Quality & Reliability Standards

A Generative Slice shall make commercially reasonable efforts to ensure all localized tools and development artifacts maintain high reliability and operational standards during scheduled working days.

4.2 Response Time & Communication

  • Working Days: Response within 2 business hours during scheduled on-site or working days.
  • Non-Working Days: Response within 24 business hours on non-scheduled days.

5. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  • Confirm scheduled weekly on-site days by the preceding Sunday via WhatsApp/Call.
